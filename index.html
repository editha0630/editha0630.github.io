About

How I Think

I believe technology is ultimately judged by the value it creates.

As an engineer developing vehicles, I loved the process of building, so much that I rarely stopped to ask what customers gained or what it meant for the business.

That question led me to strategy. Now I ask who it’s for before what we can build, especially in traditional industries where AI and software are reshaping physical products.

I still learn by making things. I just start with why.

  • Start from value
  • Ask in the field
  • Build small
